# Telecom-Customer-Churn-Analytics-Dashboard

## Project Overview
<img width="1533" height="846" alt="Screenshot 2026-07-09 092212" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0562a03c-3104-43a8-93b0-750e294c4b91" />
This Power BI project analyzes customer churn behavior and identifies key factors affecting customer retention. The dashboard provides actionable insights into customer demographics, contract types, billing patterns, internet services, and technical support usage.

The goal is to help businesses understand why customers leave and develop effective retention strategies through data-driven decision-making.

---

## Dashboard Pages

### 1️⃣ Customer Churn Intelligence Dashboard

Provides a high-level overview of customer churn metrics and business performance.

#### Key Metrics
- Total Customers: 1,000
- Total Revenue: 1.40M
- Total Churned Customers: 883
- Churn Rate: 88.3%

#### Insights Included
- Churn by Gender
- Churn Distribution
- Churn by Tenure Group
- Churn by Contract Type
- Interactive Filters:
  - Gender
  - Contract Type
  - Internet Service

---

### 2️⃣ Demographic Breakdown & Behavioral Trends

Focuses on customer demographics and behavioral patterns associated with churn.

#### Visualizations
- Customers by Age and Churn
- Monthly Charges by Age and Churn
- Monthly Charges by Tenure
- Customers by Contract Type

#### Interactive Analysis
- Gender Filter
- Contract Type Filter
- Churn Filter
- Age Filter
- Tenure Filter

#### Key Objective
Identify customer segments with higher churn risk based on age, tenure, and service usage patterns.

---

### 3️⃣ Billing Trends and Service Performance Overview

Analyzes financial performance and service-related factors affecting customer retention.

#### Visualizations
- Revenue by Contract Type
- Customers by Billing Category
- Churn by Revenue Group
- Churn by Technical Support
- Customers by Internet Service
- Monthly Charges vs Tenure Analysis

#### Business Value
Helps identify:
- Revenue-driving customer segments
- Impact of technical support on churn
- Relationship between billing behavior and retention
